Privacy Policy

Loan Debt Service Hub · Last updated October 13, 2026

Who we are

Loan Debt Service Hub ("the app") is a desktop app for a team that manages commercial real-estate loans and properties. This policy explains what personal information the app handles and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: your full name, your email address, the group (organization) you asked to join, and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our database provider; nobody can read it.
  • Your group's data: the loans, properties, documents and notes your group enters in the app, and a record of each change (who made it, when and the reason given).
  • Chats with the app's assistant, which members of your group can read.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in, and to show your group's data only to members of your group.
  • To send the emails the app needs to run your account: confirming your email address, a code to set a new password, telling a group's admin that someone asked to join (with buttons to approve or turn it down), and telling a person their request was turned down.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or send marketing email.

Sending email through Google

The app sends its emails from its own Gmail account (noreplyldsh@gmail.com) using Google's Gmail API, with permission only to send email. It does not read, store or change any mailbox, and it does not access the Google account of anyone who uses the app.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your information

  • Members of your group see your name and the changes you make; your group's admin also sees your email address.
  • The company that maintains the app can access the database only to run and support the service.
  • Service providers that host the app's data (Supabase, a database hosting service) and send its email (Google) process it on our behalf.

Keeping and deleting

Your account and your group's data are kept while your group uses the app. Daily backups are kept for 30 days. You can ask your group's admin to remove you from the group, or write to us to delete your account.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and the database. Each group's data is separated from every other group's, and every rule about who may see or change what is enforced on the server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
