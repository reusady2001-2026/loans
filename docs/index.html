Loan Debt Service Hub

A desktop app for a team that manages commercial real-estate loans and properties.

What it does

The team keeps its loans, properties and their documents in one place: payment schedules, coverage and sizing, operating statements, rent rolls and refinancing. A team works from one shared copy of its data, and every change records who made it, when and why.

Accounts and email

People join their organization's group by creating an account in the app; the group's admin approves each request. The app sends email only to run those accounts:

  • to confirm a person's email address;
  • a code to set a new password;
  • to tell a group's admin that someone asked to join, with buttons to approve or turn it down;
  • to tell a person their request was turned down.

It sends no newsletters and no advertising.
